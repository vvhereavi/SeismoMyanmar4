Our Mission

  • A Resilient Nation: Spearhead Myanmar’s transformation into a fully informed, spatially empowered, and earthquake-resilient country.
  • Data-Driven Governance: Establish geodynamic intelligence as the operational foundation for all territorial planning, urban design, civil engineering standards, and humanitarian frameworks.
  • Universal Access: Ensure every administrative level—from national policy directorates to rural township response units—has seamless access to predictive hazard models and seismic analytics.
  • Minimized Impact: Eliminate preventable human casualties, structural damage, and socio-economic disruption caused by tectonic events.

Our Vision

  • Technical & Spatial Leadership: Serve as Myanmar’s institutional spatial knowledge hub and technical authority for mitigating earthquake vulnerabilities.
  • Standardized Intelligence: Deliver multi-layered spatial analytics aligned with international humanitarian protocols and Myanmar Information Management Unit (MIMU) standards.
  • Stakeholder Empowerment: Provide actionable spatial tools and policy guidance to civil defense authorities, INGOs, urban planners, and local emergency management teams.
  • Infrastructure & Community Defense: Bridge critical information gaps to safeguard lives, secure infrastructure, and strengthen readiness along the Sagaing Fault and secondary active fault systems.
