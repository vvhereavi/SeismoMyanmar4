Demographic & Geographic Context

Country Overview

Myanmar is the second largest country in Southeast Asia with a total land area of 676,578 square kilometers with tropical monsoon climate. In the north, east and west, a rugged landscape in the Himalayan foothills of the country. It shares borders with 5 of the world's population: to the north and northeast with the People's Republic of China; to the east and southeast with the Lao People's Democratic Republic (Lao PDR) and Thailand; and to the west and northwest with Bangladesh and India.

Myanmar's 1,930-kilometre coastline provides access to sea routes and shipping ports. The economy is dominated by agriculture and farming, forestry and fishing-related activities account for almost half of the gross domestic product (GDP) in 2020. Myanmar is also one of the world's most disaster-prone countries, with high vulnerability to natural hazards, and as such, it is ranked 2nd out of 180 countries most affected by extreme weather events.

While the total population density is relatively low across the country, south East Asia, this mask a wide variation with main pocket of this population living in rural areas and the larger urban population centers like Yangon and Mandalay.

Since 2021, Myanmar is grappling with a compound socio-economic crisis, compounded by political instabilities. On top of the existing measures of the COVID-19 pandemic, an estimated 17.6 million people require humanitarian assistance, including 1.5 million displaced children and 1.1 million women, IDPs reported in the second most crash-stricken country globally according to the INFORM Risk Index of 2024.
