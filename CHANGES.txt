SeismoMyanmar — Multi-page website
===================================

Project structure (each piece in its own file/folder):
  index.html                — Home page (static map)
  risk-resilience.html      — Risk & Resilience (INTERACTIVE map — clicking a
                              region reveals its colour-coded risk level:
                              Very High / High / Moderate / Low)
  data-database.html        — Data and Database (INTERACTIVE map — clicking a
                              region updates KPI cards + intensity table)
  risk-evaluation.html      — Risk Evaluation (static map)
  country-information.html  — Country Information (static map)
  about-us.html             — About Us — Mission and Vision are now TWO
                              separate rectangles (Mission with bullseye icon,
                              Vision with eye icon), each with its own header
                              and a coloured left-border accent

  css/styles.css            — all custom CSS (map styles, social icons,
                              risk pills, risk meter, scrollbar, dark cards)
  js/main.js                — shared JS — now uses REAL Myanmar region
                              polygons sourced from Natural Earth 10m admin-1
                              GeoJSON, projected to the website's SVG viewBox
                              (0 0 400 650) using an equirectangular
                              projection that preserves aspect ratio.
                              Naypyidaw (MM-18, missing from Natural Earth
                              10m) is drawn manually at its real lat/lon.
  images/
    favicon.svg             — site favicon (red circle + cracked-house icon)
    avatar-zth.svg          — placeholder avatar for Zay Thura Htet
    avatar-syte.svg         — placeholder avatar for Shwe Yee Thoon Eain
    avatar-zbmh.svg         — placeholder avatar for Zwe Bhone Man Htet
    README.txt              — notes on how to swap in real team photos

Improvements in this version:
 1. Map polygons — replaced the old ugly square/abstract polygons (with
    messy gaps between regions) with REAL Myanmar geographic shapes from
    Natural Earth 10m data. All 15 states/regions now form a single
    contiguous country outline with proper internal borders, just like
    the reference image you provided.
 2. About Us page — the single "Mission & Vision" rectangle is now split
    into TWO separate rectangles: "Our Mission" (with a red left-border
    accent and bullseye icon) and "Our Vision" (with a blue left-border
    accent and eye icon).
 3. Footer — circular social-media icons (Facebook, Twitter/X, Instagram,
    TikTok) with brand-coloured hover effects, on every page.
 4. Interactivity — only risk-resilience.html and data-database.html have
    clickable maps. All other pages render display-only maps
    (pointer-events: none, default cursor, no tooltip, no click handler).
 5. Risk & Resilience — clicking a region updates a colour-coded risk pill
    (Very High=red, High=orange, Moderate=amber, Low=green), a sliding
    "Risk Level Meter" indicator, the region's fault zone, max magnitude,
    population, vulnerabilities, and a per-region description.

To use:
  Open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
  Tailwind CSS, FontAwesome and the Inter font load from CDNs, so an
  internet connection is required on first load.

Navigation:
  The nav bar at the top of every page is a set of plain <a href> links,
  so each page is independently bookmarkable and the browser's Back button
  works normally. The active page is highlighted in red on the nav bar.

    JavaScript: name (min 2 characters), email format, message
