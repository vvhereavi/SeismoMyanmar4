Seismic Risk Context

SeismoMyanmar provides reliable, data-driven earthquake information and safety resources across Myanmar. Located in a seismically active region crossed by major fault systems like the Sagaing Fault, the country faces significant risks from ground shaking capable of damaging buildings, roads, and critical infrastructure.

By offering interactive regional hazard maps, population metrics, historical records, and emergency preparedness guidelines, the platform empowers local communities, researchers, and planners to recognize potential risks, make informed decisions, and enhance structural safety. Ultimately, SeismoMyanmar aims to protect lives, safeguard property, and deliver practical guidance during and after an earthquake.
